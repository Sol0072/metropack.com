    How we make it

    From artwork to finished bag.

    We buy high-quality film, then print it, laminate it and make it into bags ourselves. No middlemen and no hand-offs: one team is responsible for your order from the first proof to the pallet.

    1. 1

      Color matching

      Your artwork is prepped and every ink is matched to your brand colors before a single roll runs.

    2. 2

      Printing

      Flexographic printing in up to 8 colors on our own presses, with sharp line work and clean, readable type.

    3. 3

      Laminating

      Printed film is bonded to a second film for stiffness and seal strength, with the print protected inside.

    4. 4

      Bag making

      Film is converted into finished bags and pouches, or wound as rollstock for your machines.

    5. 5

      Shipping

      Packed and palletized to protect the print, then shipped to your dock on the agreed date.

    Capabilities

    Built around your product and your line.

    We print and convert purchased film. We don’t make film ourselves, so we’re free to choose the right film for each job and focus on what matters to you: print quality and a bag that runs well on your equipment.

    Artwork & proofs

    Flexo printing in up to 8 colors. We prepare your files for press and send a proof to approve before anything runs.

    Film & structure

    Clear, white and metallized polyethylene and polypropylene films, alone or laminated together. All FDA-compliant for food contact.

    Closures & features

    Zippers added in house, plus tear notches, hang holes, windows and peel-and-seal flaps.

    Specialty coatings

    Anti-fog, UV-resistant, cold seal and anti-static coatings for products that need them.

    Line-ready supply

    Wicketed stacks, loose bags or rollstock set up to your machine’s specs.
