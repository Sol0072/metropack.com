    Custom Printed Quad-Seal Pouches

    Custom printed quad-seal pouches are sealed down all four vertical corners. Those seals keep the edges crisp and the gussets deep, so the bag holds a clean, structured shape even when it is full and heavy.

    • Four corner seals
    • Deep side gussets
    • Resealable zipper option
    Request a Quote1-800-642-0165

    Typical applications

    Whole-bean and ground coffee is the classic use, along with flour, grains, rice, pet food, birdseed and other dry goods sold by weight. The structure carries heavier fills while keeping a premium look on the shelf, and because the bag stands square it also packs neatly into cases.

    Film structures

    Quad-seal bags are usually laminated for strength and stiffness: a printed polypropylene or polyethylene outer film bonded to a polyethylene sealant layer. Metallized film gives coffee bags their classic opaque look, while clear or white film suits rice, grains and birdseed.

    Printing and features

    The front, back and both gussets can be printed in up to 8 colors, and the corner seals frame each panel with a sharp edge. Add a resealable zipper or tear notches, plus matte, gloss or registered varnish effects. For birdseed or pet food displayed in sunny stores and garden centers, a UV-resistant coating helps keep the colors from fading.

    Sizes

    Width, gusset depth and height are set to your fill weight and to how you want the bag to look standing up or lying flat. Send the product and a target weight and we will recommend a size.

    Quad-Seal Pouches FAQ

    What is a quad-seal pouch?

    A gusseted bag sealed along all four vertical corners. The extra seals add structure and give the bag sharp edges and four clearly defined panels.

    Can quad-seal pouches be resealable?

    Yes. We add a press-to-close zipper in house, so customers can close the bag again after each scoop.

    Are quad-seal pouches good for heavy products?

    They are a strong choice for heavier dry goods, because the corner seals and gussets spread the load and help the bag keep its shape.
