    Printed Wicketed Poly Bags

    Our printed wicketed poly bags are side-weld bags: cut and sealed along the sides as they are made, which leaves clean, strong edges. Stacked on a wire wicket, they open one after another on a bagging line, which is why food packers count on them for speed.

    • Matched to your bagger
    • Clean side-weld seals

    Where wicketed bags are used

    Bakery items, produce, frozen foods, ice, food service portions and everyday retail goods. Anywhere bags are filled in volume, wicketed bags help the crew or the machine move faster.

    Film choices

    Side-weld bags are made from polyethylene film selected for clarity, stiffness and how it handles on your bagger. Clear film shows the product, while white film hides it or makes the print pop. Our films are FDA-compliant for food contact, which matters for bakery, produce and frozen lines. For fresh produce, an anti-fog coating keeps condensation from clouding the bag, so greens and berries stay visible.

    Printing

    Brand graphics, product names, barcodes and handling instructions are flexo printed onto the film in up to 8 colors before the bags are made, with inks matched to your existing packaging. Clear areas can be left in the design so the product shows.

    Wickets, lips and sizes

    Bags come wicketed or loose. For wicketed bags we match the wicket style, hole pattern, lip and bag dimensions to your equipment so the bags feed cleanly. Tell us your machine and we will take it from there.

    Why Metro

    Because we print and make bags in the same Brooklyn plant, a new size or artwork change does not mean coordinating two vendors. Expect your bags about 3–4 weeks after artwork approval.

    Wicketed & Side-Weld Bags FAQ

    What is a wicketed bag?

    A stack of bags held together by a wire wicket through holes in the lip. The bags tear off one at a time, so filling is fast by hand or on a machine.

    Do you offer anti-fog film?

    Yes. We can add an anti-fog coating to produce and fresh-food bags. It keeps moisture from beading inside the bag, so the product stays clear and appealing in the cooler.

    Do you make side-weld bags without wickets?

    Yes. Side-weld bags can be supplied loose and packed flat if you fill by hand.

    What do you need to know about my bagging machine?

    The make and model, or the wicket style, hole spacing and lip size from a current bag. A sample of a bag that already runs well on your line is the easiest way.
